# Reference: What a Good Guide Looks Like

This is an example of ideal output from `/lectio-divina`. Use it to evaluate quality, pacing, and tone. The output should always be a single fenced markdown code block.

---

````markdown
```markdown
# Lectio Divina: John 13:3–9
*Maundy Thursday · NRSVue · ~30 min*

---

## The Passage

Use **[John 13:3–9 (FNV)](https://www.bible.com/bible/3633/JHN.13.FNVNT)** — Jesus rises from supper, lays aside his outer garments, and begins to wash the disciples’ feet. This is a fitting Maundy Thursday passage because it shows Jesus’ authority expressed through humble love. 

> Jesus, knowing that the Father had given all things into his hands and that he had come from God and was going to God, got up from the table, took off his outer robe, and tied a towel around himself. Then he poured water into a basin and began to wash the disciples' feet and to wipe them with the towel that was tied around him.
>
> He came to Simon Peter, who said to him, "Lord, are you going to wash my feet?" Jesus answered, "You do not know now what I am doing, but later you will understand." Peter said to him, "You will never wash my feet." Jesus answered, "Unless I wash you, you have no share with me." Simon Peter said to him, "Lord, not my feet only but also my hands and my head!"


## Begin with grounding *(~3 minutes)*
Before reading, take a moment to settle your body.

- Find a comfortable position — feet flat on the floor, hands resting open in your lap.
- Take a slow breath in through your nose, and let it out through your mouth. Do this twice more.
- Notice the weight of your body in the chair. Feel the floor beneath your feet. Let your shoulders drop.
- Bring awareness to whatever you're carrying from the day — the noise, the to-do lists, the unfinished thoughts. You don't need to put those down permanently. Just set them gently to one side, the way you might set a coat on a hook when you come inside.
- Place one hand over your heart or abdomen and quietly and pray, 

> Jesus, make me present to you.”

You are welcome exactly as you arrived.

---

## Invitation to Presence

- What is your body telling you as you arrive here?
- Where do you feel tension, resistance, or openness right now?
- What would it be like to receive this passage with your whole self, not just your thoughts?

We gather tonight on the edge of something holy — a meal, a basin, a betrayal, a love that would not stop. Before we read, we simply open. We are not here to master the text. We are here to be met by it.

Come as you are.


---

## Lectio — Read *(~4 minutes)*

Read the passage slowly, three times all the way through. Between each reading, rest in silence.

**Prompts**:
- What word, image, or moment caught your attention — something that shimmered, snagged, or surprised you?
- Don't analyze it yet. Just notice what it was.

---

## Meditatio — Meditate *(~8 minutes)*

Read the passage again, more slowly. Stay with the word or image that found you.

- Jesus takes off his robe and kneels. Peter recoils. Where do you find yourself in this scene — at the basin, or pulling back?
- Is there something in your life right now that you've been reluctant to let be tended to — a place where receiving feels harder than giving?
- What does Peter's protest — *"You will never wash my feet"* — sound like in your own voice?

*Allow the silence to hold what arises. You don't need to resolve anything.*

---

## Oratio — Pray *(~5 minutes)*

Read the passage a third time, listening for an invitation to respond. 

- What are you asking Jesus to reveal about himself to you?
- Where is Jesus inviting you to let go of control or self-protection?
- What do you need to hear from Jesus if you are tempted to say, “You will never wash my feet”?
- What do you want to say to God in light of what you've received?
- It might be gratitude, resistance, longing, confusion, or simply: *"I'm here."*

*Let your prayer be honest rather than composed. God is not grading it.*

---

## Contemplatio — Rest *(~4 minutes)*
Sit in silence with the passage.

- Release the words now. No questions, no conclusions.
- Simply be with the One who knelt.

*[Silence.]*

---

## The Passage Online

Read or listen to John 13:3–9 in NRSVue on Bible.com:
[bible.com/bible/2016/JHN.13.NRSVue]()

---

## Sharing *(~3 minutes)*

If you'd like to share something that arose — a word, an image, a question, a feeling — you're warmly invited to do so. There's no obligation and no wrong answer.

**[Name], would you be willing to go first?**

*[Others may share as they feel moved. No crosstalk or advice — just witnessing.]*

---

## Closing Prayer

God of the basin and the towel,
thank you for a love that does not wait to be deserved.
We leave this space not with all our questions answered, but held —
held by the one who knelt before us,
who knew exactly where he had come from and where he was going,
and chose, in that knowing, to wash feet.
Carry us gently into the rest of this night.
Amen.
````
